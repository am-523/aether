# DESIGN.md — Aether Hero Section (Awwwards Caliber)

## Brief & Identity
- **Subject**: Aether — AI-driven Market Intelligence & Topic Discovery Engine.
- **Tagline**: "Every Question Leads Somewhere New."
- **Sub-tagline**: "Discover emerging topics, untapped opportunities, and the conversations shaping tomorrow."
- **Scope**: Pixel-faithful hero section matching the user's provided UI reference and dreamlike flying whales background.

---

## Concept Card
- **Idea**: Surreal atmospheric serenity meets high-velocity enterprise intelligence.
- **Metaphor**: Navigating the deep uncharted oceans of data as seamlessly as skyborne whales gliding through an endless atmosphere.
- **Signature Moment**: 
  - Floating dark-glass capsule navbar with smooth magnetic hover states.
  - Kinetic typography reveal for the headline and subtitle.
  - Interactive CTA pairing: crisp white exploration pill + vibrant coral-red tactile play button.
  - 6 interactive tool integration badges (Anthropic Claude, Google Analytics, Datadog, BigQuery, Miro, Vercel) with floating magnetic lift.
  - Peek-up bottom intelligence dashboard card with interactive search bar, keyboard shortcut (`⌘K`), live date picker, and collaborator avatars.
- **Motion Verb**: "Soar, settle, and pulse" — gentle ambient atmosphere with crisp interactive snaps.

---

## Art Direction System
- **Background**: High-definition surreal turquoise ocean seascape with rocky karst islands, giant pale celestial moon, and airborne whales (`hero-bg.jpg`).
- **Color Palette**:
  - Sky/Sea Cyan-Teal: `#208b9b` / `#166574`
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF` (100% white with subtle text-shadow for legibility)
  - Text Secondary: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`
  - Glass Pill Background: `rgba(18, 38, 44, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`
  - CTA Coral-Orange: `#F34A23` / `#E63E17`
  - Dashboard Card: `#FFFFFF` with soft drop-shadow `0 24px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`
- **Typography**:
  - Headline Font: `"Plus Jakarta Sans"` / `"Outfit"`, weights 700 & 800, tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`).
  - Body / UI Font: `"Inter"`, weights 400, 500, 600.
  - Monospace / Shortcuts: `"Space Mono"`, for `⌘ K` and timestamps.
